Our Origins & Values

Indore's Trusted Property Advisory & Mandate Partners

Nakoda Real Estate is a modern real estate consultancy modeled after firms like floor.estate. We do not construct buildings; we advise homebuyers, represent quality developer mandates, and bring institutional transparency to Indore property transactions.

Our Story

Why We Built Nakoda Real Estate

The Indore real estate ecosystem is brimming with potential, but plagued by misleading super built-up claims, aggressive push sales, and fragmented brokerage channels.

Nakoda Real Estate was created to offer an independent, transparent alternative. Rather than being tied to concrete supply or individual construction sites, we partner with top developers across Nipania, AB Bypass, Super Corridor, and Vijay Nagar under structured mandate marketing agreements.

For homebuyers, this means 100% unbiased advisory, 0% brokerage on primary purchases, and multi-developer comparative analyses. For developers, we serve as an accountable sole mandate sales engine.

Our Advisory Philosophy

Client-First Advocacy & Zero Conflict

A real estate purchase is usually the largest financial decision of a family's lifetime. It should never be rushed or misrepresented.

We evaluate every development before taking it to market: Does it have clear legal title? Is the developer financially sound? Are carpet area declarations accurate under MP RERA? Is the pricing aligned with genuine market fundamentals?

If a project fails our 40-point verification checklist, we do not recommend it. Our reputation is built on long-term client trust, not one-off commissions.

How We Work

Our Standards of Service

1. 0% Buyer Commission: On all direct developer partner bookings, homebuyers pay zero brokerage fees.

2. Independent Title Diligence: Pre-scrutinized encumbrance certificates, master plan approvals, and RERA disclosures for every project.

3. Integrated Banking Desk: Priority home loan processing with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank with zero paperwork stress.

4. NRI Remote Concierge: Comprehensive virtual walkthroughs, POA guidance, and NRE/NRO banking assistance for overseas clients.
