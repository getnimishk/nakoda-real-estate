# Strategic Blueprint: Nakoda Real Estate (Indore Property Advisory & Consulting)

## 1. Business Model & Positioning (Aligned with Floor.Estate)

**Nakoda Real Estate** is an independent **real estate advisory, property consulting, and mandate marketing firm** based in **Nipania, Indore, Madhya Pradesh**. 

Unlike a single construction developer, Nakoda Real Estate **does not own or build projects directly**. Instead, exactly like **[floor.estate](https://floor.estate/)**, Nakoda Real Estate acts as a trusted advisory bridge between property seekers, property owners, investors, NRIs, and reputable Indore builders.

### Core Value Proposition
- **"Simplifying Real Estate in Indore."**
- We help individuals, families, investors, and businesses **buy, sell, lease, and invest** in residential and commercial properties across Indore.
- **Zero Brokerage for Homebuyers** on verified fresh developer bookings and mandate projects.
- **Unbiased Market Guidance**: Evaluating properties across structural quality, legal compliance, RERA registration, and realistic price-to-value metrics.

---

## 2. Service Architecture (Mirroring Floor.Estate)

1. **Buy Property (Residential & Commercial)**:
   - Curated apartments (2, 3, 4, 5 BHK), penthouses, independent villas, and residential plots in prime micro-markets: **Nipania, Vijay Nagar, MR-10, MR-11, Super Corridor, and Eastern Bypass**.
2. **Sell Property (Owner Resale & Liquidity)**:
   - Valuation, professional marketing, network matching, and verified buyer sourcing with zero hassle for property owners.
3. **Home Loan Assistance**:
   - Tie-ups with premier financial institutions (SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Bank of Baroda).
   - Lowest interest rates, doorstep documentation pickup, and speedy loan sanctions.
4. **Builder Consulting & Project Mandates**:
   - Strategic sales, branding, marketing, and channel partner management for Indore developers seeking guaranteed absorption.
5. **Commercial & Corporate Leasing**:
   - Office spaces, IT park floor plates, high-street retail shops, and built-to-suit commercial premises.
6. **Investment & NRI Advisory**:
   - High-yield pre-leased commercial assets, early-stage residential launches, portfolio management, and transparent remote transaction management for overseas Indians.

---

## 3. High-Value Consumer Tools

- **Interactive Indian Rupee EMI Calculator**: Real-time slider calculations for loan amount, tenure, and interest rates with amortization breakdowns.
- **Locality Insights & Transit Matrix**: Deep-dive comparisons of Nipania, Vijay Nagar, and Super Corridor infrastructure.
- **1:1 Property Consultation**: Frictionless appointment scheduling for private site visits and personalized portfolio recommendations.
