The Nakoda Advantage

Six Pillars of Our Advisory Model

We represent your interests at every step — from initial search and title scrutiny to price negotiation, home loan sanction, and final possession walkthroughs.

Pillar 01

0% Buyer Brokerage on New Launches

For all developer partner launches and primary market residential bookings, buyers pay zero commission. You receive institutional-grade advisory, negotiation assistance, and priority unit allotment at direct developer prices.

Pillar 02

Independent Due Diligence & Vetting

We do not accept every project. Our in-house legal and technical teams scrutinize RERA registrations, encumbrance certificates, master plan zoning, and builder delivery track records before recommending any property.

Pillar 03

True Carpet Area Transparency

No confusion over inflated "super built-up" calculations. We provide verified carpet area measurements, usable deck dimensions, and transparent price-per-square-foot comparisons across competing Indore developments.

Pillar 04

Doorstep Home Loan Processing

Direct tie-ups with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank. Our mortgage desk arranges home visits, compares rate structures (repo-linked vs. fixed), handles paperwork, and accelerates sanctions with zero processing friction.

Pillar 05

Micro-Market Mastery

Unrivaled local intelligence on Nipania, AB Bypass, Super Corridor, MR-10, and Vijay Nagar. We analyze master plan growth vectors, upcoming metro stations, and rental yield trajectories to safeguard your capital.

Pillar 06

Lifecycle Advisory & NRI Support

Our engagement does not end at booking. We assist through possession snagging, tenant acquisition, rental agreements, property management for overseas NRIs, and timely capital exit advisory.
