Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Scope & Purpose

Nakoda Real Estate ("we", "our", or "us"), operating in Nipania, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, values your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and protect personal information submitted when you interact with our website, request project brochures, or schedule a residential site visit.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information voluntarily provided by you through our website inquiry forms or direct communication channels, which may include:

  • Your Full Name
  • Mobile Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Interested Project and Residential Configuration (e.g., 3 BHK, 4 BHK)
  • Preferred Site Visit Date and Time
  • Specific requirements or messages provided in text areas

3. How Your Information is Utilized

Your information is used exclusively for legitimate real estate inquiry fulfillment:

  • To coordinate and confirm your scheduled site visit to our Nipania properties.
  • To provide architectural floor plans, pricing sheets, and project brochures upon request.
  • To contact you via phone call, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding your expressed interest.

4. Zero-Sale Commitment & Backend Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing brokers or telemarketers. Customer inquiry submissions are securely channeled directly into our private Google Workspace / Google Sheets lead record system. We do not store sensitive personal information in public code repositories or client-side browser cookies.

5. Opt-Out & Contact

If you wish to update your details or request that our advisory team cease contacting you, please notify us directly via email at nbrproperty@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp with the word "STOP".
