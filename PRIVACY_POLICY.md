# Privacy Policy: Nakoda Real Estate

**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Operating Location:** Nipania, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India  
**Entity:** Nakoda Real Estate & Advisory

---

## 1. Overview
Nakoda Real Estate ("we", "our", "us") values consumer trust and privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how customer inquiries, site visit bookings, and communication preferences are collected, used, and secured through our static digital web platform.

---

## 2. Customer Information Collected
We collect information only when explicitly submitted by the user through our website forms, direct phone calls, or WhatsApp communication:
- Full Name
- Indian Mobile Contact Number
- Email Address (Optional)
- Interested Project (e.g., Nakoda Solitaire, Nakoda Terraces)
- Configuration Preference (e.g., 3 BHK Luxury, 4 BHK Royal Grand)
- Preferred Site Visit Schedule (Date and Time slot)
- Specific inquiry messages or notes

---

## 3. Use of Personal Information
Submitted data is utilized strictly for:
1. Contacting you to confirm and coordinate property walkthroughs and site visits in Nipania, Indore.
2. Sharing verified floor plans, price breakdowns, and project brochures.
3. Facilitating direct home loan underwriting coordination upon buyer request.

---

## 4. Zero Data Commercialization & Backend Security
- **No Third-Party Sale:** We never sell, lease, or distribute customer lead data to external marketing agencies, call centers, or property brokers.
- **Backend Flow:** Data is transmitted directly to our secure Google Form / Google Sheets workspace repository. No consumer data is stored in unencrypted client-side browser cookies or public code repositories.
